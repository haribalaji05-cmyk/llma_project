Passport Application Process in India — Overview

Passport services in India are managed by the Ministry of External Affairs through the Passport Seva Programme. 
Citizens can apply for a fresh passport, renewal, or reissue of a lost or damaged passport. 
Applications must be submitted through the official Passport Seva portal at passportindia.gov.in.

Step 1 — Register on the Passport Seva portal. Create an account using a valid email address and mobile number. 
Step 2 — Fill the online application form (Form SP-1 for a normal passport or Form SP-2 for a diplomatic passport). 
Step 3 — Pay the applicable fee online using net banking, debit card, or credit card. 
Step 4 — Schedule an appointment at the nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK). 
Step 5 — Visit the PSK on the appointment date carrying originals and self-attested photocopies of all required documents. 
Step 6 — Biometric data (photograph and fingerprints) will be collected at the PSK. 
Step 7 — Application is forwarded to the Regional Passport Office (RPO) for police verification and final processing. 
Step 8 — Passport is dispatched to the registered address via Speed Post once approved.

Official portal: https://www.passportindia.gov.in
Helpline: 1800-258-1800 (toll-free)

Passport Application — Required Documents

All applicants must carry original documents along with one self-attested photocopy at the time of the PSK appointment.

Proof of Date of Birth (any one):
- Birth certificate issued by the Municipal Authority or Registrar of Births and Deaths
- Transfer/School leaving certificate from the last school attended
- Matriculation certificate
- PAN card (for adults)
- Aadhaar card

Proof of Address (any one):
- Aadhaar card
- Voter ID card
- Water / Electricity / Telephone (postpaid) bill (not older than 3 months)
- Registered rent/lease agreement
- Bank/Post Office account passbook with current address
- Income tax assessment order

Proof of Identity (any one):
- Aadhaar card / e-Aadhaar
- Voter ID card
- PAN card
- Driving licence

For Minors — additional documents:
- Birth certificate is mandatory
- Passport copy and Aadhaar of parent/guardian
- Declaration by parent(s) in Annexure H if parents are separated or divorced

For Government Employees:
- Identity certificate issued by the employer (Annexure B)
- No-objection certificate from the department concerned

Passport Application — Processing Time and Timelines

Normal (Ordinary) Passport:
- Processing time is typically 30 to 45 working days from the date of application, subject to police verification completion and RPO workload.
- After successful police verification and document check at the RPO, the passport is printed and dispatched via India Post Speed Post.
- Applicants receive SMS and email updates at each stage: application received, police verification scheduled, police verification done, passport dispatched, and delivered.

Tatkal (Urgent) Passport:
- Tatkal applications are processed within 1 to 3 working days at the PSK level.
- Post-police verification, final dispatch generally takes 7 to 14 working days.
- Tatkal requires the submission of additional supporting documents to justify urgency (e.g., medical emergency, employment abroad).

Tracking:
- Applicants can track passport status at: https://www.passportindia.gov.in
- Track using the file reference number (FRN) received on the appointment receipt.
- SMS alerts are sent at each stage to the registered mobile number.

Helpline: 1800-258-1800 (toll-free, available Monday–Friday, 8 AM to 10 PM)

Passport Application — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can I apply for a passport without an Aadhaar card?
A: Yes. Aadhaar is not mandatory but is accepted as proof of identity and address. Other valid documents can be submitted.

Q: Is police verification required for all passport applications?
A: Police verification is required for most first-time (fresh) applications. For renewal of passports with no change in personal information, post-verification (after dispatch) may apply.

Q: Can a minor apply for a passport independently?
A: No. A minor's passport application must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian. Both parents must provide consent (Annexure H if one parent is absent or estranged).

Q: What is the validity of a minor's passport?
A: A passport issued to a minor below 18 years is valid for 5 years or until the minor turns 18, whichever is earlier.

Q: Can I use the passport as address proof for other documents?
A: Yes. A passport is accepted as a valid proof of identity and address by most government agencies in India.

Q: What should I do if my passport is lost or stolen?
A: File an FIR with the local police immediately and obtain a copy. Then apply for reissue on the Passport Seva portal with the FIR copy and Annexure F.
