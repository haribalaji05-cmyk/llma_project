Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) — Overview

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) administers the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS), and Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme (EDLI) in India under the EPF and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

EPF applies to all establishments with 20 or more employees in specified industries. Both the employee and the employer contribute to the provident fund every month.

Contribution Rates:
- Employee contribution: 12% of Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance (DA)
- Employer contribution: 12% split as — 3.67% to EPF + 8.33% to EPS (capped at Rs. 1,250 per month)
- Government contribution to EPS for employees earning up to Rs. 15,000 per month: 1.16%

Each employee is issued a Universal Account Number (UAN) which remains the same throughout their career even when they change jobs.

Benefits of EPF:
- Tax-free interest on accumulated corpus (8.25% p.a. for 2023-24 — verify on epfindia.gov.in)
- Pension (EPS) after 10 years of continuous service
- Life insurance cover under EDLI (up to Rs. 7 lakh)
- Partial withdrawal for medical, housing, education, and marriage needs

Official portal: https://www.epfindia.gov.in
Member portal: https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in
Helpline: 1800-118-005 (toll-free)

EPF — Universal Account Number (UAN) Activation and KYC

The Universal Account Number (UAN) is a 12-digit number allotted to every EPF member by EPFO. It is generated by the employer when an employee joins a covered establishment for the first time.

Steps to Activate UAN:
1. Visit the EPFO Member portal: https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in
2. Click 'Activate UAN'
3. Enter your UAN, registered mobile number, date of birth, and member ID (from salary slip or employer)
4. An OTP will be sent to the mobile number linked with EPFO
5. Enter the OTP and set a new password to activate your account

KYC (Know Your Customer) Update:
Once activated, members must link KYC documents for smooth withdrawal and transfer requests:
- Aadhaar (mandatory — enables e-KYC and auto-approval of claims)
- PAN card (for tax purposes on withdrawals)
- Bank account with IFSC code (for direct credit of withdrawals)

KYC is updated via the Member portal and must be approved by the employer in the Employer portal.

Seeding Aadhaar with UAN:
- Log in to EPFO member portal → Manage → KYC → Add Aadhaar → Save
- Employer verifies and approves

Note: Aadhaar-seeded and employer-verified UAN accounts allow online claim filing without employer intervention for certain claim types.

Source: https://www.epfindia.gov.in

EPF — Withdrawal Rules and Online Claim Process

Types of EPF Withdrawals:

1. Full Withdrawal (Final Settlement):
 - Allowed after 2 months of unemployment (resignation, termination, retirement)
 - At retirement (age 58)
 - Claim forms: Form 19 (EPF) + Form 10C (EPS) + Form 31 (if applicable)

2. Partial Withdrawal (Advance):
 - Medical emergency: Up to 6 times monthly wages + DA or employee share with interest (no service limit)
 - Marriage / Education: Up to 50% of employee's share (minimum 7 years service)
 - Housing (purchase / construction): Up to 90% of EPF balance (minimum 5 years service)
 - Home loan repayment: Up to 90% of EPF balance (minimum 10 years service)
 - Claim form for advance: Form 31

Online Claim Filing (if UAN is Aadhaar-seeded and KYC complete):
1. Log in to https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in
2. Go to Online Services → Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C, 10D)
3. Verify bank account details
4. Select claim type and submit
5. Claim is processed within 20 working days; amount is credited directly to the bank account

Claim Tracking:
SMS: EPFOHO UAN ENG to 7738299899

Source: https://www.epfindia.gov.in

EPF — PF Transfer Process When Changing Jobs

When an employee changes jobs, the EPF balance can be transferred from the old employer's PF account to the new employer's PF account using the UAN. This ensures continuous service (important for EPS pension eligibility).

Online PF Transfer (Form 13 online):
1. Log in to EPFO Member portal: https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in
2. Go to Online Services → One Member – One EPF Account (Transfer Request)
3. Verify your personal details and previous member ID
4. Select either previous employer or current employer to attest the claim
5. Submit the transfer request online
6. Track status on the portal

Important Points:
- UAN must be active and KYC (Aadhaar + PAN + bank) must be verified by the employer
- Previous member ID (PF account number with old employer) is required
- Transfer request is processed within 20 working days once approved by the employer

Pension Transfer (EPS):
- EPS balance is transferred automatically along with EPF transfer
- Scheme Certificate is issued for service less than 10 years to preserve pension entitlement

Avoiding Multiple PF Accounts:
- Always provide UAN to every new employer to link the new PF account to the same UAN
- Unlinked accounts can be merged using the EPFO composite claim form

Source: https://www.epfindia.gov.in

EPF — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What is the current EPF interest rate?
A: EPFO declares the interest rate annually. For 2023-24, it is 8.25% per annum. Check https://www.epfindia.gov.in for the latest declared rate.

Q: Can I check my EPF balance without the UAN?
A: Yes, using your member ID. But activating your UAN is recommended for full online access.

Q: How do I check my EPF balance?
A: Options include:
 - EPFO Member portal: https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in
 - SMS: EPFOHO UAN ENG to 7738299899
 - Missed call: 011-22901406 (from registered mobile only)
 - UMANG app

Q: Is EPF withdrawal taxable?
A: EPF withdrawal is tax-free if the member has completed 5 years of continuous service. Withdrawals before 5 years attract TDS (10% with PAN, 34.608% without PAN).

Q: What happens to EPS if I leave before 10 years of service?
A: You can withdraw the EPS amount using Form 10C, or obtain a Scheme Certificate to preserve the service record for future employment.

Q: Can a self-employed person join EPF?
A: No. EPF is for employees of covered establishments only. Self-employed individuals can consider PPF (Public Provident Fund) as an alternative.
