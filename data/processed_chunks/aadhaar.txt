Aadhaar — Overview and Importance

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identification number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) to residents of India. It serves as proof of identity and proof of address for all Indian residents, regardless of age or gender.

Aadhaar captures biometric data (fingerprints and iris scans) and demographic data (name, date of birth, gender, address, mobile number, and email). It is used for:
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of subsidies and welfare benefits
- Filing income tax returns
- Opening bank accounts (via eKYC)
- SIM card activation
- Government scheme enrolment (ration card, MGNREGA, PM-KISAN, etc.)
- PAN-Aadhaar linking (mandatory for income tax purposes)

Aadhaar is voluntary as per the Supreme Court of India judgment (2018), but is required for availing specific government benefits and subsidies. UIDAI is the statutory authority constituted under the Aadhaar Act, 2016.

Official website: https://uidai.gov.in
Helpline: 1947 (toll-free)

Aadhaar Enrolment — Step-by-Step Process

Who can enrol: Any resident of India, including children (even newborns — Baal Aadhaar is issued with blue colour for children below 5 years).

Step 1 — Locate a nearby Aadhaar Enrolment Centre using the UIDAI website: https://uidai.gov.in or the mAadhaar app.

Step 2 — Visit the enrolment centre without an appointment (walk-in). Carry original documents for identity proof and address proof.

Step 3 — Fill the Aadhaar Enrolment / Correction Form at the centre. Centre operators will assist.

Step 4 — Submit biometric data:
- Photograph
- Ten fingerprints
- Iris scan
(Children below 5 years are exempt from biometrics; re-enrolment is required at age 5 and 15.)

Step 5 — Receive an Acknowledgement Slip with a 14-digit Enrolment ID (EID). This is used for tracking status.

Step 6 — Aadhaar is generated within 90 days and sent to the registered address via post. It can also be downloaded from the UIDAI portal using the EID.

Enrolment is FREE of charge at official Aadhaar Seva Kendras.
Track enrolment status: https://uidai.gov.in/en/my-aadhaar/check-aadhaar-status.html

Aadhaar Enrolment — Required Documents

Proof of Identity (any one):
- Passport
- PAN card
- Voter ID card
- Driving licence
- NREGA job card
- Certificate of identity with photo issued by a Gazetted Officer or Tehsildar
- Arms licence

Proof of Address (any one):
- Passport
- Bank / Post Office passbook with photo
- Voter ID card
- Ration card
- Driving licence
- Government-issued address certificate
- Electricity bill / Water bill / Telephone bill (not older than 3 months)
- Property tax receipt

Proof of Date of Birth (for DOB update):
- Birth certificate
- SSLC / Matriculation certificate
- PAN card
- Passport

For Head-of-Family (HoF) Based Enrolment (for family members without individual documents):
- Aadhaar of the head of family is required
- Relationship proof such as ration card, MNREGA job card, or marriage certificate

For Children below 5 years (Baal Aadhaar):
- Birth certificate
- Parent's or guardian's Aadhaar

Aadhaar Update — How to Update Your Aadhaar Details

UIDAI allows residents to update the following details in their Aadhaar:
- Name (minor corrections allowed)
- Date of birth (once in a lifetime only)
- Gender
- Address
- Mobile number
- Email address

Online Address Update:
- Visit https://ssup.uidai.gov.in (Self Service Update Portal)
- Log in using your Aadhaar number and registered mobile OTP
- Upload a scanned copy of valid address proof
- Pay a fee of Rs. 50 for demographic update

Mobile Number / Email Update:
- Must be done at an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre or Aadhaar Seva Kendra as biometric authentication is required for mobile number updates.

Offline Update:
- Visit a nearby Aadhaar Enrolment Centre or Aadhaar Seva Kendra
- Fill the Aadhaar Enrolment / Correction Form
- Submit supporting documents
- Fee: Rs. 50 per update request

mAadhaar App:
- Available on Android and iOS
- Allows address update, profile management, and Aadhaar lock / unlock

Important: Demographic updates can be made a limited number of times. Verify all details carefully before submitting.

Source: https://uidai.gov.in

Aadhaar — Key Online Services and Frequently Asked Questions

Key Online Services (available at uidai.gov.in):
- Download e-Aadhaar (PDF): Free, password-protected with your name and birth year
- Verify Aadhaar number
- Lock / unlock biometric data (to prevent misuse)
- Virtual ID (VID) generation — a 16-digit temporary ID to use instead of Aadhaar number
- Check Aadhaar Authentication History
- Order Aadhaar reprint (Rs. 50 + Rs. 25 postal charges)

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Is Aadhaar free to obtain?
A: Yes. Aadhaar enrolment is completely free of charge at government-designated centres.

Q: Can an NRI get Aadhaar?
A: Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) with a valid Indian passport may apply.

Q: My mobile number is not registered with Aadhaar. Can I still use online services?
A: No. Many online UIDAI services require an OTP sent to the registered mobile number. Visit an enrolment centre to update your mobile number.

Q: Is PAN-Aadhaar linking mandatory?
A: Yes, for filing income tax returns. Check the Income Tax Department portal for the current deadline.

Q: How do I lock my Aadhaar to prevent misuse?
A: Log in to the UIDAI portal or mAadhaar app and use the Lock / Unlock Biometrics feature.
